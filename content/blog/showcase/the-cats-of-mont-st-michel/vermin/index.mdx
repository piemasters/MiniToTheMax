> The game features a cast of unique playable characters, each with their own specialized classes—such as Fighters,
> Healers, and Assassins—represented by distinct player boards and custom skill cards

The box contains a total of 17 villians being mostly cockroaches and rats, each with their own unique abilities.
The vermin are:

- Arbitrators (The Snipers): These specialize purely in ranged combat. They only have a single hit point, meaning they die easily if a hero can close the distance, but they carry powerful crossbows. They will systematically stay at a distance and pick off your heroes from across the room using ranged sightlines.
- The Crimson Cardinal: The villainous leader orchestrating the entire abbey invasion. As the ultimate campaign boss, the Cardinal uses a strict priority-based artificial intelligence sheet to dictate his turns. He is highly durable, starts the fight with a powerful shielding mechanism, and will prioritize casting a mass Heal power the moment he or any other adjacent boss vermin takes damage.
- Detestables (The Toxic Threat): These foul units rely on chemical warfare. They radiate a toxic stench that causes a massive problem for your party. If a hero strikes a Detestable with a melee attack while standing nearby, it triggers an immediate, nasty area-of-effect status penalty or wound trigger. Dealing with them safely heavily requires a ranged strategy from magic users or rifle-wielders.
- L'Abesse (The Abbess): A high-ranking, sinister religious figure among the rodents who has taken over spiritual dominion of the abbey. She is a manipulative tactician who managed to usurp other prominent vermin to sit directly at the Crimson Cardinal's right hand.
- Maximilien: Named in a distinct nod to the French Revolution era (Maximilien Robespierre), he is the ruthless, fanatical military general of the rat infestation. He commands the frontline execution of the Cardinal's plot with absolute martial authority.
- Muscadin de Precy: A haughty, remarkably well-spoken rat who was formerly the Crimson Cardinal's trusted advisor. After being unceremoniously replaced by L'Abesse, he was demoted to commanding a wretched regiment of cockroaches. Nicknamed "La Bouche" (The Mouth), he is completely fearless and utterly determined to ruthlessly regain his former glory.
- Roaches (The Swarm): These are basic, easy-to-kill pests meant to overwhelm you by sheer numbers. While they lack complex attack mechanics, they are highly aggressive. If a cockroach successfully manages to move into the exact same map space as one of your heroes, it automatically inflicts a wound on the cat and is then removed from the board.-
- Norwegian Brown Rats (The Bruisers): These are the frontline muscle of the rodent army. They are large, high-health heavy-hitters that possess multiple hit points, making them difficult to take down in a single blow. They deal substantial melee damage and have card effects that further empower them when fighting in close quarters.

I did some pretty quick paint jobs on these like I did with the heroes as once again as I wanted to get them on the
table to play the game, so I didn't make note of the paints I used.

I really like the characters, as well as the rats, but I'm not the biggest fan of the cockroaches. I think they are a
bit too simple and don't have enough character to them, but I guess they are meant to be the fodder.

The quality of the prints or casts isn't ideal like with the heroes, and some of the longer thin bits are very prone to
warping. Despite my usual trick of heating them up with the kettle and bending them back into shape, then submerging
them in cold water to set, much is it slowly warped back. The boxes and padding they came in is far too small, and even
after carving out the foam the staff on the cardinal is too long so is forced to warp in the box.

As for the paint jobs, again I think they all came out pretty good for a bit of a rushed job. I used the same varnish
as I did with the heroes, and again I think it came out a bit too glossy for my liking.
