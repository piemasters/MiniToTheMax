> The game features a cast of unique playable characters, each with their own specialized classes—such as Fighters,
> Healers, and Assassins—represented by distinct player boards and custom skill cards

The box contains a total of 9 playable heroes and companions, each with their own unique abilities and playstyles.
The heroes are:

- Adara (The Wizard): A vibrant calico wizard. She commands powerful spellcraft and unique magic mechanics to deal area-of-effect damage and manipulate scenarios from a distance, supporting the front-line fighters.
- Athena (The Monk): A tiny owl monk with highly unconventional, specialized stats. Athena brings aerial tactical support, unique movement capabilities, and precise martial arts to the battlefield.
- Friedrich (The Fighter): A dachshund dog fighter who breaks the feline mold. Armed with direct physical power, he acts as a stalwart companion to the cats, absorbing damage and protecting the squishier magic-users.
- Guy (The Rogue): A heavily scarred, gray tabby rogue. Guy is the quintessential scavenger and thief of the group, specialized in disarming traps, slipping past vermin undetected, and looting high-tier gear from cleared rooms.
- Leon (The Musketeer / Paladin): A large, clever, and handsome young cat following in the paw prints of his father and brother—both legendary musketeers. In gameplay, he functions as a high-mobility swashbuckler. At Level 3, he can unlock an incredibly powerful skill that doubles his action points and adds a bonus to all hit rolls, allowing him to zoom across the map and clear out enemies.
- Nera (The Ninja / The Shadow who Bites): A mysterious Siamese voyager from far-off Nippon. Shrouded in secrecy, she tracks down the former agents of the Rat King. Mechanically, she acts as the team's high-damage assassin, striking from the shadows to deal devastating critical wounds.
- Sekhmet (The Cleric): An Abyssinian cleric. She acts as the primary healer of the party. One of her highly discussed Level 3 abilities allows her to exhaust her card to heal all allies in her line of sight while simultaneously dealing free, no-roll damage to all adjacent enemies without consuming action points.
- Stumpy (The Barbarian): A fierce ginger barbarian cat. Stumpy trades heavy finesse for pure brute force, boasting high health tracks and devastating melee modifiers meant to stun or wound enemies instantly on high D12 rolls.
- Tiers: An unusual cooperative hero unit consisting of a group of three mice working together. They offer distinct multi-model mechanics, distracting larger vermin and squeezing into tight spaces where larger cats cannot fit.

I did some pretty quick paint jobs on these heroes as I wanted to get them on the table to play the game, so I didn't
make note of the paints I used. Overall I like the sculpts and think my quick paint jobs came out well enough, although
I think the quality of the prints could have been better and the final varnish I applied came out a bit too glossy for
my liking. I think the miniatures would have looked better with a matte finish, but I can always fix that in the future.
