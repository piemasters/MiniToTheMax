## Miniature Review

I purchased this miniature from Siege Studios, painted to their Silver level. I've been to a number of classes with
James and have always admired the quality of the company's work. I really wanted either a Silver or Gold level miniature
so that I could reference and learn from it when painting my own pieces.

It's one thing to see photos online, but having the miniature in front of you really helps with understanding the
techniques that were used and how the overall finish looks compared to my current standard. The main thing I notice is
just how thin and smooth the layers are and how well the transitions are blended.

I need to study the gems and lenses more as they look so natual having the reflections and highlights in the
right places to give the illusion of depth. I also really like how some of the corner highlights are maybe a bit
exaggerated when looking closely, but from a distance really help to separate the materials and give certain materials
an extra shine.
