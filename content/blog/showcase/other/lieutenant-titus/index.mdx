> Lieutenant Demetrian Titus has a well-earned reputation for decisive action and incredible skill in combat. He has
> fought countless battles in the Emperor's name and always emerged victorious despite seemingly impossible odds. From
> fighting against the brutish Orks on the forge world of Graia, to cutting a gory path through Tyranid invasion swarms
> amidst the jungles of Kadaku and across the hive spires of Avarax, Titus has faced and defeated the foes of Humanity
> wherever duty demands.

On a black base coat, I first used White Scar through the airbrush to create a zenithal highlight.
I then used Luxion Purple through the airbrush in all the shadow areas to add some vibrance and some additional
interest over a darker blue or black.
I reinforced the zenithal highlight with more White Scar through the airbrush, then airbrushed Ultramarine Blue over the
midtones and highlights.
Once more I reinforced the zenithal highlight with White Scar, then airbrushed Talassar Blue to the brightest
highlights.
I then highlighted the edges from thick lines of Macragge Blue all the way through to spot highlights of Blue Horror.
