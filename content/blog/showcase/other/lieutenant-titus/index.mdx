> Lieutenant Demetrian Titus has a well-earned reputation for decisive action and incredible skill in combat. He has
> fought countless battles in the Emperor's name and always emerged victorious despite seemingly impossible odds. From
> fighting against the brutish Orks on the forge world of Graia, to cutting a gory path through Tyranid invasion swarms
> amidst the jungles of Kadaku and across the hive spires of Avarax, Titus has faced and defeated the foes of Humanity
> wherever duty demands.

## Miniature Review

I really enjoyed the Space Marine game years ago when it came out and I'm really glad that they were able to make a
second more recently and that it's done so well there will likely be a third in the future.

There are currently two miniatures of Titus, Lieutenant Demetrian Titus pictured in this article, and the newer
Captain Titus. For me the older miniature much more resembles the feel of Titus in the game, constantly in combat.

I like the pose of charging forwards, but I feel like something is slightly off as he seems a bit stiff. It might just
be the angle of his arms. The face is great and has so much detail, having barely painted any primaris marines I'm
always surprised how much more details the faces have now compared to the firstborn.

On a black base coat, I first used White Scar through the airbrush to create a zenithal highlight.
I then used Luxion Purple through the airbrush in all the shadow areas to add some vibrance and some additional
interest over a darker blue or black.
I reinforced the zenithal highlight with more White Scar through the airbrush, then airbrushed Ultramarine Blue over the
midtones and highlights.
Once more I reinforced the zenithal highlight with White Scar, then airbrushed Talassar Blue to the brightest
highlights.
I then highlighted the edges from thick lines of Macragge Blue all the way through to spot highlights of Blue Horror.

## Roundup

This was a fairly quick paint job as I just needed a palette cleanser, but I'm really happy with how it came out. The
armour lighting looks pretty good for how much effort I put into it, the only downside is a lot of the purple shadow
has been lost. The face looks a bit rough in the zoomed in photo but looks good when viewing in person.

Overall it was fun to paint but I think I'll be sticking with Space Wolves for now!
