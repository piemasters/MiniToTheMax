> Fellwater Troggoths make their lairs in turbid rivers, foul marshes, and crumbling sewers, feasting on unfortunate
> passers-by. These slime-slick beasties stink worse than a plague pit, and throw up jetting streams of viciously
> corrosive vomit to soften up their prey - quite literally - before tearing into them with wicked talons and
> creekwood clubs.

## Miniature Review

I never had the original River Trolls, just the Rock Trolls, but despite releasing almost 20 years after the originals
it's now been over 15 years since these sculpts were released and they're starting to show their age compared to the
other troll sculpts.

They're very squat and fat, but do have a lot of character, especially their faces. I love the one being sick, and the
fish being held is a great detail too. The mix of skin and scales helps to break up some of the largest areas, but I
would have liked more of the barnacles or something else to help break up some the largest empty areas.

### Skin

To start I painted each troll a different base color. One was Militarum Green, one Mantis Warriors Green and the last
Plaguebearer Flesh. This helped give them some variety, but after washes and highlights they all come together nicely.

I then applied a watered down mix of Coelia Greenshade and Garaghaks Sewer to each troll. This helped to bring out the
details and blends them together. The brown added to the green gives them a muddier look which fits the swampy theme.

I then drybrushed highlights onto the skin. Starting with Yriel Yellow, then Lugganath Orange and finally Astorath Red
on areas like the knuckles, nose, lips and bum.

### Scales

<Paint name="Dark Angels Green" />
<Paint name="Coelia Greenshade" />
<Paint name="Garaghaks Sewer" />
<Paint name="Niblet Green" />
<Paint name="Hellion Green" />

As with the skin the Coelia Greenshade and Garaghaks Sewer were watered down and mixed together and applied as an all
over wash. Garaghaks Sewer is a strong paint so make sure not to use too much of it as it will overpower the green.
Once dry I highlighted the scales with Niblet Green and then Hellion Green.

## Roundup

I'm not sure how much I like these trolls now I've finished them. I really enjoyed the colors I used for the skin and
scales, but I think the vibrancy doesn't really work with everything else. Maybe if I toned the skin down to be much
more drab they would have looked better, or maybe I needed to tone down other areas. It's hard to tell exactly what
is wrong with them, but maybe one day I can come back and filter a certain area down to improve them.
